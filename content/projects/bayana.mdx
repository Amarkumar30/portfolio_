## Problem

For an independent wedding photographer, a booked date is that day's income. If the booking lives only in a chat or a phone call, it isn't really locked — for the vendor or for the couple.

## What I'm building

Bayana gives independent vendors a simple way to confirm a booking properly:

- The client pays an **advance** over UPI (through Razorpay) to lock the date.
- Both sides get a **written record** of what was agreed.

## Where it's starting

Wedding photographers in Delhi NCR first, then other independent wedding and event vendors.
