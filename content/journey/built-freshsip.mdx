At a busy juice bar counter, orders came in by phone and in person, and payments were confirmed by glancing at a phone screen. I built FreshSip on my own: customers order and pay by UPI, and staff track every order on a live dashboard.

The hard part was keeping payment state and order state in agreement, so an order reaches the counter only once its payment is confirmed.
