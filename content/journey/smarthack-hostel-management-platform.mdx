A team project at SmartHack: one place for hostel room allocation, complaints and policy questions. I worked across the stack, mostly on authentication, the REST APIs and real-time complaint updates.

The hardest part was anonymous complaints that still need follow-up. Storing a salted hash of the student's identity, instead of the identity itself, let wardens act on a complaint without seeing who filed it.
