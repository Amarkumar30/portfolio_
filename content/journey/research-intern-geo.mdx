It started with something small. My roommate bought a water purifier by asking ChatGPT one question, and never opened a website. I wanted to know why the model picked that brand. That question became my research internship at SAU.

Two ideas are in progress: Hinglish-GEO-Bench, which tests whether an LLM's brand recommendation shifts when the same question is asked in Hinglish, and the Indian Brand Visibility Score (IBVS), a measurable score for a brand's presence in generative answers.
