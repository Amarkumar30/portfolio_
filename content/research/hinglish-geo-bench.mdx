## The question

Does an LLM's brand recommendation shift when the same query is asked in Hinglish instead of English?

Most writing on Generative Engine Optimization assumes one language and one way of asking. Indian users rarely ask that way. A real query looks like *"purifier chahiye jo hard water handle kare, budget kam hai"* — half Hindi, half English, no punctuation. I couldn't find a benchmark that tests brand recall and recommendation in that register.

## Why it matters

If answers change, or lean further towards large English-first brands, when the question is in Hinglish, then Indian D2C and regional brands may be less visible in AI-driven discovery simply because of how their customers talk. That would be an unfair problem, and a fixable one.

## Approach

Building this properly means:

- **Paired queries** — the same intent written in English and in Hinglish.
- **A query-generation pipeline** to produce those pairs at scale.
- **An evaluation harness** that sends them to several answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini) and records which brands are mentioned, and how.
- **A scoring rubric**, and probably a small annotated dataset built by hand, because off-the-shelf tools don't handle code-mixed Hindi-English well yet.
