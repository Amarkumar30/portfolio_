## The question

How do you measure whether a brand exists to ChatGPT?

In classic search, there are numbers people trust: rankings, traffic, domain authority. In generative search there is nothing equivalent. A founder at a D2C skincare brand or a regional fintech app has no idea whether AI answer engines mention them at all.

## The idea

The **Indian Brand Visibility Score** is an attempt at a single, measurable score for a brand's presence across generative answer engines — the way a domain authority score summarises presence in classic search.

## Why it's an engineering problem

Visibility has to be measured across several model providers, each with different retrieval behaviour, training cutoffs, and tendencies to cite or hallucinate. That makes IBVS a data-pipeline and evaluation problem before it's a marketing one.
